Getting found

Built to show up on Google.

Google ranks pages, not websites. The more good pages you have answering real searches, the more ways you get found. Here is what is already in place, in plain language.

  • One page per search"Tamarindo fishing charter", "35 Cabo Flamingo", "sunset catamaran Tamarindo", "Rio Celeste from Tamarindo". 90 pages means 90 doors into your site instead of one.
  • Titles and descriptions on every pageEach page tells Google exactly what it is and what a searcher will get. That is the text that shows up in the results list.
  • Structured data Google can readYour business, address, hours, phone, star rating, and the price of every boat are written in Google's own format. This is what lets prices and stars appear right in the search result.
  • Sitemap and robots fileA map of all 90 pages handed to Google directly, so it indexes everything instead of finding pages by accident.
  • Fast loadingSpeed is a ranking factor. 444 photos were compressed to a third of their size, video is streamed only when it is on screen, and there is no bloated platform underneath.
  • Phone-first layoutGoogle judges your site by its phone version. This one was built for phones and then widened, not the other way round.
  • Secure address (https)The padlock in the browser. Google prefers it and guests trust it.
  • Descriptions on every photoGoogle cannot see pictures. Each photo has a written description, which also puts you in Google Images for "sailfish Tamarindo".
  • Clean sharing on WhatsApp and FacebookWhen a guest shares a boat page, it shows the boat photo and rate, not a broken link.
  • Google Business Profile, set up and polishedThe map listing with reviews, photos and hours. This is what shows up first for "fishing charter Tamarindo" on a phone. Included, I do it for you.
  • Google Search Console connectedThe free dashboard that shows which searches bring people in. I set it up and send you the login.
  • Old links redirectedAt launch, every address from the old site points to the matching new page, so nothing you have earned on Google is lost in the move.
Honest note. Nobody can promise a number one spot, and anyone who does is selling you something. What I can promise is that this site gives Google everything it asks for, which is more than most charter sites in Guanacaste do, and that your fifteen years of reviews and blog posts now sit on a foundation that can carry them.
