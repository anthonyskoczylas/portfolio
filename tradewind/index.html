A day with Tradewind

From first message to five-star review.

Here's how one job moves through Tradewind, using a sample landscaping customer named Dana.

  1. 8:05 am

    A lead comes in

    Dana fills out the form on your website with three photos of her backyard. Your phone buzzes, and a reply is already written. You read it, tap send, and you're the first one to answer.

  2. 12:30 pm

    The quote goes out

    On your lunch break you build the quote from your price book: patio, base prep, tax and a deposit. Or let AI draft it from your own prices and just check it. Then text her the link.

  3. 3:15 pm

    She signs from her phone

    Dana opens the link and approves by typing her name. The signed quote is locked with the date and time. No printing, no scanning, no "did you get my email?"

  4. 3:20 pm

    It's on the calendar

    You schedule the job and assign your crew. She wants weekly mowing too, so you set it to repeat every week. The visits fill in on their own until you stop them.

  5. Job day

    The invoice gets paid

    Your crew taps "On my way", checks off the list and snaps before and after photos. Invoice link sent. When she pays by Zelle, you record it in a tap and she drops off your "who owes me" list. If she forgets, a reminder is already written.

  6. Next morning

    The review gets asked

    A thank-you with your Google review link is written and waiting. One tap sends it while she's still happy with the work.

And every Monday, a money report is waiting for you.

Questions

Straight answers.

Something else on your mind? Message me on WhatsApp and I'll answer it myself.

Do I need a Coastal CR website to use Tradewind?

Yes. Tradewind is connected to the website I build you. That's what sends every request straight into your app, and what lets you change your site from your phone without retyping anything.

Does the AI send messages on its own?

No. It writes drafts and puts them in front of you. Nothing goes to a customer until you read it and tap send. When it drafts a quote, it only uses prices from your own price book. It never makes up a price.

Can customers pay by card?

Not yet. Card payments and autopay are coming soon. Today you send an invoice with a link, take cash, check, Venmo or Zelle the way you already do, and record it in Tradewind so you always know who still owes you.

Is there an app in the App Store?

The iPhone app is coming soon. Right now Tradewind works on any phone and on your computer, and you can add it to your home screen so it opens just like an app.

Can my crew use it?

Yes. Each crew member gets their own login with limited access. They see their jobs, checklists and photos, and never the money.

How does texting work?

When you send a quote or message by text, your phone's messages app opens with it ready to go, so it comes from your own phone. Automatic texting from your business number is coming soon, including missed-call text-back and two-way texts in the app.

Can I set up repeat jobs?

Yes. Set any job to repeat weekly, every 2 weeks or monthly, and the visits fill in on your calendar. You can stop repeating any time.

Can I change my own website?

Yes. Change your photos, services, hours and service areas, hit Publish, and your site updates in under a minute.

Is my data safe?

Your business's data is walled off from every other business that uses Tradewind, connections are encrypted, and everything is backed up regularly. If you forget your password, you can reset it yourself.

What does it cost, and is there a contract?

$2,000 to build your custom site when you pay up front, then $300 a month: the $200 care plan plus $100 for Tradewind. You can also split the build or start small, the same 3 ways to pay as my website plans. Tradewind itself has no minimum: either of us can end it any time with one message. When it ends, the app logins for you and your crew are switched off, you get a spreadsheet of your customers, quotes and invoices, your website quote form goes back to sending requests to your email, and your plan drops back to $200 a month. The care plan keeps its 6-month minimum.
