Questions

Straight answers.

Something else on your mind? Message me on WhatsApp and I'll answer it myself.

What is Tradewind?

Tradewind is Coastal CR's CRM partner. Its ready-made app, Tradewind Trades, connects to the website I build you, and I set it up and stay your one point of contact. For other kinds of businesses, Tradewind builds custom CRMs: see tradewindcrm.com.

Who is Tradewind Trades for?

Trade and home service businesses: landscapers, cleaners, painters, pressure washing, pools, pest control, handymen and contractors. If your business works differently, Tradewind Custom is the better fit.

Do I need a Coastal CR website to use Tradewind Trades?

Yes. Tradewind Trades is connected to the website I build you. That's what sends every request straight into your app, and what lets you change your site from your phone.

Can customers pay by card?

Yes. Customers pay their invoice by card from the link you send, and the money goes straight to your own Stripe account. You can still record cash, check, Venmo or Zelle.

What does it cost, and is there a contract?

$2,000 to build your custom site when you pay up front, then $300 a month: the $200 care plan plus $100 for Tradewind Trades. You can also split the build or start small, the same 3 ways to pay as my website plans. Tradewind Trades has no minimum: either of us can end it any time with one message, and your plan drops back to $200 a month. The care plan keeps its 6-month minimum.
