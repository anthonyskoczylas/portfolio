What is included

  • Hosting and securityYour site lives on fast, free hosting with a secure address. Nothing to update or patch, and I keep a full backup of every version.
  • Domain renewalI renew your domain every year and keep it in your name. Standard domains, about $20 a year, are included. Unusual ones are billed at cost.
  • Changes, up to 2 hours a monthPrices, hours, photos, wording, a seasonal promo, a new review, a menu update. Send it, it gets done. Hours do not roll over. Anything bigger is quoted first.
  • TurnaroundMost changes within 24 hours on business days. A wrong price or a site that is down gets fixed the same day, any day.
  • Uptime watchAn automatic check pings your site around the clock. If it ever goes down, I know before you do.
  • One Google Business post a monthA photo and a few lines on your Google map listing, which keeps the listing active. Send me something, or I pick from the site.
  • A yearly refreshOnce a year we look at photos, wording and speed together and freshen what has aged.
  • Advice on requestAdding a new tour? Changing prices? Ask. Short answers are free and part of the plan.

What is not included

  • New pages, big new sections or a full redesign. Quoted first, usually small.
  • Writing a blog, running ads, or social media beyond the one Google post.
  • Photography and video.
  • Third-party costs: booking systems, paid tools, Google Ads budget, premium domains.
  • Print work such as cards and flyers. Separate quote.

How to ask for a change

  1. Message me on WhatsAppText, a voice note, or a screenshot with a circle on it.
  2. I confirm and give a timeSmall things land the same or next business day.
  3. I send you the link when it is liveNot right? Say so and I adjust.

Money

  • From $200 a month. Your exact amount is on your proposal and does not go up without a new agreement.
  • Pay monthly in advance, or yearly in advance for the price of 10 months. Two months free.
  • Zelle, Venmo, PayPal, SINPE Móvil or bank transfer. A receipt after every payment.
  • Due on the same day each month or year as your start date. Seven days of grace, no late fees.

Ending the plan

  • Cancel any time with a message. Monthly: the plan ends at the end of the paid month. Yearly: unused full months are refunded at the monthly rate.
  • If you stop, your site stays yours and stays online. Hosting is free for life. Changes are then quoted per request, and the domain renewal becomes yours to pay.
  • Starting again later is fine. No setup fee.

What is yours

  • The website, its files, its domain, its photos and words, and your Google listing are yours, care plan or not. Ask and I hand over everything within a week.
  • A small "site by Coastal CR" line stays in the footer. Ask and I remove it.
Plain-language terms, not legal small print. If anything here is unclear, ask before you pay, and if we ever disagree, the answer that is fairest to you wins.
