Questions

Straight answers.

What is the difference between Basic and Custom?

Basic is one page for $500, with one-tap WhatsApp contact and your Google Business setup, then a $50 a month care plan for hosting, your domain and small edits. Custom is a multi-page site with online booking, and it comes with the $200 a month care plan: unlimited changes, usually the same day, plus hosting, domain renewal and a watch on the site around the clock.

Who owns the website?

You do. The website, its files, its domain, its photos and words, and your Google listing are yours, care plan or not. On Small start, that is once the 12 payments are made. Ask and I hand over everything within a week.

Is there a minimum?

Every care plan, Basic or Custom, has a 6-month minimum from launch day. After that it is month to month and you can cancel with one message. Tradewind Trades has no minimum: either of us can end it any time with one message, and the plan drops back to $200 a month. Growth plans have a 3-month minimum.

What happens if I stop the care plan?

After the minimum, your site stays yours and stays online, and hosting is free for life. Changes are then quoted per request, and the domain renewal becomes yours to pay. Starting again later is fine, with no setup fee.

How fast are changes?

Most changes are live the exact day you ask. A wrong price or a site that is down gets fixed right away, any day. With Tradewind you can also change photos, services, hours and service areas yourself and publish in under a minute.

I already have a website I like. Do I need a new one?

No. A website transfer rebuilds it exactly as it is, on your same address, from $500, and then it is on the care plan. If you want it improved rather than copied, that is a new site.

Do you guarantee more bookings?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. Nobody controls Google. What I promise is the work listed for your plan, done every month, and growth plans come with a report with real numbers so you can see for yourself if it is paying off.

What does it cost to find out what I need?

Nothing. Every project starts with a free first look. Send me your business and I will tell you honestly which package, if any, is worth it for you.
