The design

Built to look like the work.

Landscaping sites usually look soft and generic. Jake's work is heavy stone, big machines and clean lines, so the site is too: condensed type you can read from across the room, deep pine greens and limestone, and a contour-map pattern drawn just for him.

Behind the scenes

The stuff you don't see.

A good-looking site that loads slowly, loses its Google rankings or drops quote requests isn't worth much. This is the part customers never notice, and the part that keeps the phone ringing.

Loads fast

Every photo is resized and compressed into two sizes, so a phone on a weak signal up north still opens pages quickly.

Old Google links still work

Every page from the old template points to its new home with a permanent redirect, so the rankings Jake already had carry over instead of starting from zero.

Google understands it

Built-in business details tell Google exactly what Costa Outdoors is, where it works and how to reach it, plus a sitemap of every page.

Privacy & terms

Proper privacy and terms pages, with texting consent built into the quote form, so follow-up texts are allowed by the book.

Same email, no downtime

Moving to the new site only touched the website records. Jake's email kept working the whole time, and the old site stayed up until the switch.

Secure

Runs on https with a certificate that renews itself, on fast, reliable hosting. Nothing for Jake to maintain.
